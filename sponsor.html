Bingo halls

Every hall’s listing is free, always. Beyond that, choose what fits your hall:

  • Community partner. Ohio’s veteran, fraternal and sporting halls must give part of their instant bingo profit to a charity. If you name Veteran Home Guardians as one of your recipients, we ask for a modest share, usually 5 to 10 percent of what you give, not all of it. At minimum, it should cover visits to veterans in nursing homes in your county. Anything beyond that helps us reach counties that have no local support. Every hall is different, so we work it out together.
  • Feature your bingo event, $100. Have a special bingo night or fundraiser? We feature it, labeled, in the local results players see near you on Find Bingo.
  • A website for your hall. If you’d rather pay for a service than partner, we’ll build and keep up a simple website for your hall, for a fee.
  • Advertising. Free tips on getting the word out, or paid advertising if you want more.

A featured event never changes or reorders anyone’s free listing.

How it works

  1. Send an inquiry. Tell us your business, the level you want and which guide fits.
  2. We confirm the details. We check the fit, agree on the wording and send an invoice.
  3. Your sponsorship goes live. We add it to the guide, labeled “Sponsored,” and send you the link.

Our sponsor guidelines

  • Every sponsored spot is labeled “Sponsored” so visitors can always tell it apart from our free listings.
  • Sponsor spots show your name, logo, link, contact details and a plain description of what you offer. They do not include prices, discounts, rankings or calls to buy.
  • Licensed businesses must hold a current license and follow their state’s rules for what they display.
  • Sponsorship never changes our free listings or who can get help from us.
  • We invoice sponsors and accept check or bank transfer.
