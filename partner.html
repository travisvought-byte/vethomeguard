Who we work with

  • Businesses and sponsors. Sponsor visits at a facility, run an employee volunteer day or a roundup campaign. To sponsor one of our guides, see sponsorships.
  • In-kind donors. Give tools, building materials, a vehicle or professional services.
  • Churches, civic and veteran groups. Team up on events, referrals and outreach in your community.
  • Care facilities. Bring Guardian visits to your residents and share our guides with families.
  • Media. Help tell the story of veterans helping veterans.
