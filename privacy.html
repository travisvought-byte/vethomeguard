Privacy

Your privacy

What this site collects, what it does not, and the choices you have. Last updated November 25, 2026.

The short version

There are no accounts and no sign-in. We only get your name, phone number or email address when you send them to us in a form or a message. We do not sell personal information.

At a glance

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • We get your name, phone number or email address only when you send them to us.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • Our forms run on Wix, which may set a few cookies of its own.
  • We do not see or store your card or bank details when you give.
  • GoatCounter counts page views without cookies.
  • This site loads Google Tag Manager to measure our nonprofit ads. Google never gets what you type in a form.
  • This site is not directed to children under 13.

Forms and messages

When you refer a veteran, volunteer, ask about a partnership or send a message, what you type goes to our account on Wix, which runs the forms, and we read it there. We use it to reply and to arrange visits, and we never sell it. Please share only what we need to reach you and the veteran. Wix may set a few cookies of its own to run the forms; read Wix’s privacy policy.

If you call or email us, we get your phone number or email address and what you tell us, and we use it the same way.

Donations

Gifts go through PayPal, Zelle or our Giving Tuesday campaign on Zeffy. We do not see or store your card or bank details. The service you use tells us your name, email address and the amount so we can thank you and send a receipt. Read PayPal’s privacy statement.

Counting visits and measuring our ads

We count page views with GoatCounter, which does not use cookies and does not store personal information. It records things like the page visited, the site that sent you here, your browser and an approximate country.

We run free search ads through Google’s nonprofit Ad Grants program. To measure them, this site loads Google Tag Manager for every visitor. If you came here from one of our Google ads, Google’s ads tag also learns whether you then sent a form, so we know which ads lead to a referral or a volunteer. Google may set cookies and receives your internet (IP) address and the pages you visit, but never what you type into a form. We turn off ad personalization signals for these measurements.

You can manage how Google uses your information at adssettings.google.com and read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Our guides

The Veteran Resource Guide and our other guides are separate sites with their own privacy pages. See the Resource Guide privacy page.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
