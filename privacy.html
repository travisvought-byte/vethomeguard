Privacy

Your privacy

What this site collects, what it does not, and the choices you have. Last updated November 23, 2026.

The short version

There are no accounts and no sign-in. We only get your name, phone number or email address when you send them to us in a form or a message. We do not sell personal information. The site shows no ads.

Forms and messages

When you refer a veteran, volunteer, ask about a partnership or send a message, what you type goes to our account on Wix, which runs the forms, and we read it there. We use it to reply and to arrange visits, and we never sell it. Please share only what we need to reach you and the veteran. Wix may set a few cookies of its own to run the forms; read Wix’s privacy policy.

If you call or email us, we get your phone number or email address and what you tell us, and we use it the same way.

Donations

Gifts go through PayPal or Zelle. We do not see or store your card or bank details. PayPal tells us your name, email address and the amount so we can thank you and send a receipt. Read PayPal’s privacy statement.

Counting visits and measuring what works

We count page views with GoatCounter, which does not use cookies and does not store personal information. It records things like the page visited, the site that sent you here, your browser and an approximate country.

We also use Google’s measurement tag (Google Analytics and Google Ads) to see how people find us through Google search and which pages lead to a referral, a volunteer form, a call or a gift. It may set cookies and receives your internet (IP) address, the pages you visit and those actions, but never what you type into a form. You can turn off ad personalization at adssettings.google.com, read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Our guides

The Veteran Resource Guide and our other guides are separate sites with their own privacy pages. See the Resource Guide privacy page.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
