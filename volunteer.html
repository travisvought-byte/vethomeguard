Volunteer

Become a Guardian

Veterans and civilians are both welcome. Help make sure veterans in nursing homes are seen, heard and not forgotten.

Ways to help

  • Guardian visitor. Make regular visits to veterans in nursing homes to talk and listen.
  • Resource guide helper. Check guide listings for accuracy and report what has changed.
  • Event helper. Help at community events that fund nursing home visits.
  • Administrative support. Help with scheduling, outreach, fundraising and coordinating with activity staff.

Scheduling is flexible, and we provide training and support.
