Refer a veteran

Know a veteran who would welcome a visitor?

Tell us about a veteran living in a nursing home or assisted living facility. A Guardian will follow up with you.

How it works

  • 1. You send a referral. Share how to reach you and where the veteran lives.
  • 2. We call you. A Guardian follows up to learn more.
  • 3. We visit. We work with the facility’s activity staff to set up a visit.

We visit in Morrow, Knox, Delaware and Marion counties. If the veteran lives somewhere else, the Resource Guide lists the county veterans office that can help.

Please share only what is needed to arrange a visit. Do not send medical details.
