Our story

Still serving, together

Veteran Home Guardians connects younger veterans and friends of veterans with senior and disabled veterans, so no veteran is left isolated.

What we do

Visits

Guardians visit veterans in nursing homes in Morrow, Knox, Delaware and Marion counties every week.

Guides

We publish free guides so veterans, seniors, families and care staff can find the right office or program. Open the Resource Guide.

Community

We work with care homes, churches, veteran posts and local businesses to reach more veterans.

How we started

Veteran Home Guardians was founded in 2025 by Travis Vought, a Marine Corps veteran, in central Ohio.

A bourbon raffle and the Live. Stay. Project, a suicide prevention nonprofit, got us started. Thank you to everyone who bought a ticket, gave or spread the word.
