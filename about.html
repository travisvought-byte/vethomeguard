Still serving, together

Veteran Home Guardians connects younger veterans and friends of veterans with senior and disabled veterans, so no veteran is left isolated.

Our mission

No veteran should be forgotten. Veteran Home Guardians makes sure veterans living in nursing homes and assisted living are seen, heard and visited, and that every veteran, senior and family can find the help they have earned.

Guardian visits

Many veterans in long-term care go weeks without a visitor who understands military life. A Guardian is a veteran, or a friend of veterans, who visits them every week to sit, talk and listen.

  1. Someone refers a veteran. A family member, friend or care home tells us about a veteran who would welcome a visitor.
  2. We match a Guardian. We follow up with the family and the care home and arrange regular visits.
  3. Guardians keep coming back. Visits happen every week, and Guardians point veterans and families to benefits and services they may be missing.

Guardians are paid for their visits, because steady visits depend on people who can keep showing up. Community sponsors fund the visits, and we keep administrative costs to no more than 10 percent so the rest goes to the visits themselves.

Today Guardians visit veterans in Morrow, Knox, Delaware and Marion counties in Ohio. Our aim is to reach every nursing home in Ohio. Refer a veteran.

Free guides

Visits reach the veterans in front of us. The guides reach everyone else. The Veteran Resource Guide lists county veterans service offices, VA benefits, health care, housing, long-term care and crisis lines in every state and the District of Columbia, and emergency and veterans contacts for veterans living in other countries.

Each listing comes from an official source, and anyone can report an entry that is out of date. The guides are free, need no sign-up and are open to everyone, veterans or not. Care homes, veteran posts and families can print a flyer to hand out.

How we are funded

Veteran Home Guardians is funded by individual donors, community sponsors and community partners, including bingo halls that name us as a charitable recipient. Sponsor and partner money pays for Guardian visits and for keeping the guides accurate and free. Sponsorship never changes a free listing or who can get help from us, and every sponsored spot is labeled. How sponsorship works.

  • Individual donors
  • Community sponsors
  • Community partners, including bingo halls

How we started

Veteran Home Guardians was founded in April 2025 by Travis Vought, a Marine Corps veteran, in central Ohio. The IRS recognized it as a 501(c)(3) public charity in December 2025.

  1. April 2025Founded in central Ohio by a Marine Corps veteran
  2. December 2025Recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3) public charity
  3. TodayGuardians visit veterans every week in four Ohio counties

A bourbon raffle and the Live. Stay. Project, a suicide prevention nonprofit, got us started. Thank you to everyone who bought a ticket, gave or spread the word.
